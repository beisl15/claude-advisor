## Nota de cobertura (narrativa, moat e valuation)

COPASA (Companhia de Saneamento de Minas Gerais) is the state-owned water and sewage utility serving 80% of Minas Gerais municipalities. The moat is a perpetual regulated concession — COPASA operates under long-dated contracts (25-35 years) with ARSESP-equivalent tariff reviews that guarantee an adequate return on invested capital. Damodaran narrative: Brazil's sanitation sector is in the middle of a mandated universalization wave — the New Legal Framework for Sanitation (2020) requires 99% water access and 90% sewage treatment by 2033, driving mandatory capex that COPASA funds through tariff resets. Scuttlebutt: COPASA has outperformed peers on operating efficiency (NRW — non-revenue water losses — declining steadily) and has a strong track record of meeting regulatory targets. Capital allocation: COPASA pays 25-50% of net income as dividends; capex is heavy (~25-30% of revenue) to meet the universalization targets. Bear case: state intervention in tariff reviews (political risk — Minas Gerais governor controls COPASA); deteriorating credit metrics if capex acceleration is not met with commensurate tariff resets. Graham safety: moderate leverage (1.5-2× ND/EBITDA), investment-grade locally, regulatory asset base fully recognized.
